FAQ

Questions executives ask us.

Why don't you publish prices?

Because no two engagements are alike. A founder who needs inbox and travel help a few hours a day needs something very different from a leadership team that needs full-time coverage. We price to your real scope, so you only pay for the support you use.

What does my Executive Partner do?

Your Executive Partner is assigned to look after your relationship with your assistant. They check in with you both, sort out issues early and learn your workflow, so they can step in as your backup whenever needed.

What happens if my assistant leaves?

Your Executive Partner already knows how you work, so they keep your support running while we match and onboard a new assistant. You don't start from zero.

How do your assistants use AI?

They use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini to research, plan workflows, find the best approach to new tasks and check they've understood your instructions. Zapier handles repeat tasks. Your assistant reviews everything before it reaches you, and only uses AI tools in ways you approve.

Can I change my support later?

Yes. As your role or business changes, tell us and we'll adjust the hours, scope and pricing to match.
